## Project Overview

A weekly meal planner with three core capabilities:
1. **Inventory management** — track pantry/fridge items, add/remove via receipt scanning
2. **Receipt scanning** — take a photo; LLM extracts items and updates inventory automatically
3. **Meal planning** — weekly suggestions referencing current inventory
